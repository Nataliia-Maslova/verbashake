🗑️ Delete your VerbaShake account

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your VerbaShake account (developer: Nataliia Maslova) and all data linked to it.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open verbashake.app or the VerbaShake app and sign in with Google.
  2. On the home screen, open the side menu (» at the top left).
  3. Open ⚠️ Delete my account (shown in your app language), type DELETE and press the confirm button.

Your data is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Option 2 — by email

Send an email from the Google address you sign in with to

📧 nataliiamaslovaneo@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my VerbaShake account

Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a confirmation email.

What is deleted

  • Your account data (name, email address) and settings.
  • All learning progress: lessons, mastery, review schedule, streaks, XP, badges, study time.
  • Everything you created: your phrases, saved words, mistakes, schedules and feedback messages.
  • Your subscription record. An active Premium subscription is cancelled.

What is kept

  • Technical error logs are kept, but with your account identifier removed, so they no longer relate to you.
  • Payment records held by Stripe are kept as long as tax law requires (usually several years).
  • Voice recordings are never stored, so there is nothing to delete.

Want to delete only some data, not the whole account? Email the address above and say what you'd like removed. See also the Privacy Policy.
