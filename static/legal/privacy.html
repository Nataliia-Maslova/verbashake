🗣️ VerbaShake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

VerbaShake is a language-learning app, available at verbashake.app and as an installable app. This page explains what data it collects, why, and who it is shared with. This policy is written in plain language on purpose; the English version is the authoritative one.

What data is collected

  • Account info — your name and email address, provided by Google when you sign in ("Continue with Google"). VerbaShake does not see or store your Google password.
  • Learning progress — which lessons you've done, how well (a per-topic "mastery" score), spaced-repetition scheduling, your chosen languages and level, streaks, XP, badges and study time — so the app can pick up where you left off and recommend what to practice next.
  • Content you type or say — answers you type into exercises, your mistakes (so you can review them later), words you save, phrases you create in "My Phrases", and texts you paste in (for example song lyrics or articles). Audio you record for speaking practice is turned into text on VerbaShake's own server and is not stored afterwards; the audio itself is never sent to Google or any other outside company.
  • Subscription status — whether you have Premium, and the Stripe customer and subscription IDs needed to check it. VerbaShake never sees or stores your card details.
  • Usage counters and error reports — e.g. how many times per day you've used an AI feature (to keep the free limits working), feedback you send through "Report a problem", and technical error logs when something breaks. These are not used for tracking or advertising.

Who your data is shared with

VerbaShake doesn't sell your data, and doesn't share it for advertising. It relies on a few outside services to run:

  • netcup GmbH (Germany, EU) — hosts the app's server, where voice transcription also runs.
  • Supabase — the database that stores your account, progress and content listed above. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google — for signing in; for Premium AI features (warm-up questions, grammar correction, explanations, translation, practice exercises, conversation practice) the relevant text you type is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response; and for text-to-speech, the phrase being read aloud is sent to Google's speech service. Your voice recordings are not sent to Google.
  • Stripe — processes Premium payments. Stripe receives your email address and payment details directly; see Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • Streamlit (Snowflake Inc., USA) — hosted an earlier copy of the app at verbashake-ai.streamlit.app, which may still run for a short transition period.

Some of these providers (Google, Stripe, Snowflake) may process data outside the EU, for example in the United States, under the safeguards required by the GDPR (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses).

Why your data is used (legal basis)

  • To provide the app and your account, and the Premium subscription if you buy one — necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • Error logs and abuse limits — our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and fair.
  • Payment records — kept as long as tax law requires.

Payments

Premium is a paid monthly subscription handled by Stripe. VerbaShake only stores whether your subscription is active and its Stripe IDs.

Cookies

Signing in with Google sets one cookie so you stay logged in between visits. VerbaShake does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from VerbaShake's database right away — see how to delete your account. Payment records held by Stripe are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask (via the contact email above) to:

  • see what data is stored about you, and get a copy of it,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • have your account and data deleted,
  • restrict or object to some uses of your data.

You can also complain to a data protection authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Children

VerbaShake is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you're a parent and believe your child has created an account, contact the email above and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date. See also the Terms of Use.
