🗣️ VerbaShake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

VerbaShake is a language-learning app. This page explains what data it collects, why, and who it is shared with. This policy is written in plain language on purpose; the English version is the authoritative one.

What data is collected

  • Account info — your name and email address, provided by Google when you sign in ("Continue with Google"). VerbaShake does not see or store your Google password.
  • Learning progress — which lessons you've done, how well (a per-topic "mastery" score), spaced-repetition scheduling, streaks, XP, and badges — so the app can pick up where you left off and recommend what to practice next.
  • Content you type or say — text you type into exercises, and audio you record for pronunciation practice. Voice recordings are transcribed to text locally, on VerbaShake's own server — the audio itself is never sent to Google, or any other outside company.
  • Custom lessons — any phrases you create yourself in the "My Phrases" module.
  • Basic usage counters — e.g. how many times per day you've used a given AI feature, used only to keep the free daily limits working, not for tracking or advertising.

Who your data is shared with

VerbaShake doesn't sell your data, and doesn't share it for advertising. It does rely on a few outside services to run at all:

  • Google — for signing in ("Continue with Google"), and, for certain premium AI features (grammar correction, translation, the AI conversation practice), the relevant text is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Your voice audio is not part of that — see above.
  • Supabase — the database provider that stores your account and progress data.
  • Streamlit Community Cloud — hosts the app itself.

These providers may process data outside your own country (for example, in the United States). They are established providers with their own data-protection safeguards; VerbaShake does not add any further sharing on top of what's needed to run the features above.

Payments

VerbaShake does not currently charge for anything — no payment information is collected today. If paid subscriptions are introduced in the future, payments will be handled by Stripe (which never shares your full card details with VerbaShake), and this policy will be updated before that happens.

Cookies

Signing in with Google sets one cookie so you stay logged in between visits. VerbaShake does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you ask for your account and data to be deleted (see contact above), it will be removed from VerbaShake's database.

Your rights

Wherever you're located, you can ask (via the contact email above) to:

  • see what data is stored about you,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • have your account and data deleted,
  • get a copy of your data.

Children

VerbaShake is not specifically directed at children under 16. If you're a parent and believe your child has created an account without your consent, contact the email above and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
